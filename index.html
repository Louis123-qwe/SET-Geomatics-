Who We Are

SET Geomatics
Consult Ltd

SET Geomatics Consult Ltd (RC: 8129539) is a duly registered geomatics and land survey consultancy firm based in Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria. Under the visionary leadership of our CEO, Surv. Todonukun John S., we deliver accurate, legally compliant, and technologically advanced survey solutions.

From boundary demarcation to engineering surveys, our team combines field expertise with modern geospatial tools to serve individuals, developers, corporations, and government bodies alike.
